How SmartClass Works

A seamless 5-step journey from classroom launch to analytical mastery.

1

Create Session

Teacher starts a live lecture session with an auto-generated 6-character room code.

2

Students Join

Students enter the room code from their laptop or mobile without tedious account barriers.

3

Launch Activity

Professor triggers an interactive poll, diagnostic checkpoint, or timed quiz.

4

Students Respond

Students submit responses instantaneously, receiving immediate feedback and grading.

5

Live Analytics

The dashboard updates in real time with participation metrics, graphs, and attendance logs.
